## 4. Workflow design

| Applicant | Days | Proposed route from current baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Staf biasa | <= 5 | Pengurus Unit (support/comment) → Pengurus Besar (final) |
| Staf biasa | > 5 | Pengurus Unit → Pengurus Besar (support/recommendation) → Setiausaha ALK (final) |
| Pengurus Unit | <= 5 | Direct to Pengurus Besar (final), no self-support (FR-20/CT-DEC-050). |
| Pengurus Unit | > 5 | Direct to Pengurus Besar (support/recommendation), then Setiausaha ALK (final); no self-support (FR-20/CT-DEC-050). |
| Pengurus Besar | any | Direct to Setiausaha ALK final, no intermediate self-support, CR-HR-2026-002. |

CT-DEC-008: negative unit support requires reason; forwards for consideration; does not reject automatically. For >5 days, preserve GM recommendation and Secretary final decision. Pengerusi ALK distinct but no GM approval under superseding decision; no access by assumption. No automatic approval, escalation or delegate. Unavailable assignee stays pending with approved reminders only. Verify exact existing SRS status labels before final transition table; do not add business statuses silently.

Proposed commands: draft/update, submit, support/not-support, final approve/reject, return-for-correction, resubmit, withdraw, request/confirm cancellation, HR record and staff confirmation, authorized balance adjustment. All actions use policy and target-level authorization, revision check, transaction and audit; action visibility in Vue is not security. Return-for-correction releases reservation only for unused leave (CT-DEC-024); actual absence requires HR review before release. Resubmit revalidates and restarts workflow; old decisions remain history. Cancellation restores original allocation only after authorized confirmation; calendar changes do not silently alter approved status. HR sees authorized oversight and adjustments but does not become extra final approver. No payroll or attendance writes.

## 5. Calculation contract

- AL and EL share the same entitlement. Separate request types, not balances. Use approved Johor calendar and working schedule; two nonoverlapping half-day sessions can total one day; reject overlap with active full-day/session. AL normal reason optional; short-notice exception requires emergency reason; EL reason required, attachment optional, retrospective window controlled by approved HR configuration. Early EL notification outside system per CT-DEC-028.
- Use approved leave-year basis, separate annual and Tabung balances, pending reservations deducted from available projection. Use AL then Tabung; authorized deficit resides in AL, not Tabung. Warn about negative projection. Current CT-DEC-002 and CT-DEC-014 HR answers explicitly allow insufficient/negative balance and set no maximum at present; warn, do not reject solely for insufficient balance. CT-DEC-020 current answer confirms all active pending included and no negative cap configuration needed. CT-DEC-018 records deficit in AL, never Tabung. A future cap needs controlled change; do not implement speculative config.
- Final approval converts hold into posted use exactly once (or pre-credit future-year commitment); ordinary rejection/valid withdrawal of unused leave releases it once. CT-DEC-029/056: rejected retrospective EL uses a distinct absence adjustment from AL then Tabung despite status Ditolak; shortfall to HR, no automatic salary/attendance classification. Do not release and debit twice. Preserve original allocation and year on reversal; reconcile post-year-close changes according to CT-DEC-039 rather than creating a second entitlement. No floating-point arithmetic or duplicate debits.
- Cap applies to Tabung only, not combined balance; cap follows annual entitlement per CT-DEC-004. Year-end transfer/cap/forfeiture, negative rollover and future-year reservation must each follow current HR answer; run idempotently with preview and audit. CT-DEC-015 allows approved early next-year allocation around 25 December, including approved current-year negative settlement; no blanket automatic offset. Exact 25 December inclusion and treatment of excess remain confirmation points (034 says mulai 25, 015 says selepas 25). No automatic payroll deduction (payroll out of scope).
- Prorata termination formula: annual entitlement / 12 × eligible months; round to nearest 0.5 per SRS, exact tie/partial-month method requires HR confirmation. Do not infer service-band boundaries or promotion adjustment; handle appointment-letter exception only with confirmed HR rule. HR review of legal minimum and final settlement is mandatory; no financial transaction generated.
